Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

Primalfront (the game at primalfront.com) is operated by Fontaine Farm SRL, a company registered in Belgium (VAT BE 1004.190.223, Brussels, Belgium). Contact: daniel@garden-stack.com.

What we collect

  • Account & gameplay data — if you create an account: your chosen name, a persistent player ID, and in-game stats. You can play anonymously without an account.
  • Technical data — IP address and basic device/browser information, used to run the service, prevent abuse, and for security.
  • Payments — if you purchase anything, payment is processed by Stripe; we do not store your card details.
  • Analytics — aggregate, privacy-respecting usage metrics to improve the game.

Why & legal basis

We process this data to provide and secure the game (contractual necessity and our legitimate interest in running and protecting the service), and to process payments you request. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us operate the game (e.g. Stripe for payments, hosting/CDN providers), bound to process it only on our instructions.

Retention

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active, and technical/log data for a limited period for security and abuse prevention, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights (GDPR)

As this service is operated from the EU, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and request portability. Email daniel@garden-stack.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

Children

Primalfront is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
