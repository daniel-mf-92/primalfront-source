📦 **Primalfront — Latest Changelog**

🎵 **New: Era Soundtrack**

- Every Age now has its own music. The score crossfades as you advance, from
  primal drums in the Primitive era all the way to eerie synths in the Alien era.

🌍 **7-Age Progression (Primitive → Alien)**

- Advance through Primitive, Imperial, Feudal, Enlightened, Industrial, Modern
  and Alien ages — each with its own units, buildings and balance.
- Ages advance on real criteria (settlements, keystones, tech points), not just
  gold, with an explicit anti-snowball / no-linear-rush design.

🗺️ **Living Map**

- Latitude-derived biomes tint structures and terrain to match the land.
- Predator tribes (Wolf, Bear, Lion, Jackal) roam, hunt and split as they grow.
- Resource nodes — wood, rock and meat — can be claimed and harvested by workers.

⚔️ **Combat & Expansion**

- Pathway / corridor territory claims and gentle rewilding curb early rushes.
- Distance-decayed expansion: contiguous land is cheap, far-flung conquest costs.

🛠️ **Fixes & Polish**

- Reworked unrest meter, hut/house placement and on-map glyphs.
- Numerous balance, rendering and performance improvements.
